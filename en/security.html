Security

A web firewall, free TLS, a friendly CAPTCHA, secure tunnels, and Zero Trust access — layered protection that turns on with a few clicks.

Security at Cloudflare comes in layers: protect public websites (WAF, SSL/TLS, Turnstile) and protect your internal networks and teams (Tunnel, Access, Gateway). You don't need to be a security expert — sensible defaults do most of the work.
